Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The Quiet Hours operates a YouTube channel and uses a private, self-hosted application (the "App") solely to upload and manage the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API. This policy explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

Information we access

  • Google account authorization tokens (OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens) for the channel owner's own Google account, granted by the owner through Google's consent screen.
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and title) needed to confirm that uploads are made to the correct channel.

The App does not collect, access, or store information about viewers, subscribers, or any other third party.

How information is used

Authorization tokens are used only to authenticate the App's uploads and video-management requests to the owner's own YouTube channel. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's own server, are never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and are never used for advertising or analytics.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens remain stored only while the App is in use. The owner may revoke the App's access at any time from the Google account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately invalidates the stored tokens; stored token files are then deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent via the channel's About page on YouTube: The Quiet Hours.
